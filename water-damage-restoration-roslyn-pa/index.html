Water Damage Restoration in Roslyn, Abington, PA

Roslyn sits at the headwaters of Sandy Run, and the creek corridor runs through the community. Whether water came from a pipe, an appliance or a heavy rain, the first steps are the same, and a call is the quickest way to start.

Water damage in Roslyn homes

Roslyn is a residential community in Abington Township with a SEPTA Warminster Line station at Susquehanna Road and Easton Road, a fire company and an elementary school. Most water damage calls from a community like this start inside the house, with a split supply line, a failed washing machine hose, a leaking water heater or a ceiling stained by a roof leak.

Wherever it starts, wet drywall, insulation and flooring begin to break down quickly, so the sooner the source is stopped and the water is out, the more of the structure can usually be kept. Our water extraction and structural drying pages explain what a crew does, and the Abington-wide picture is on our water damage restoration in Abington page.

Where Roslyn sits and what the township says about it

Most of Roslyn is in Abington Township, though the community also extends into Upper Dublin Township. The Abington part is in the Abington School District. If you are unsure which township you are in, check your tax bill, because permit and utility questions go to the township that serves your address.

Abington Township's planning documents describe the Sandy Run floodplain as running from Roy Chester Park through Crestmont and Roslyn to the Upper Dublin boundary, and say parts of it are preserved in Grove Park and Roslyn Park. Those documents also describe lethal flooding and extensive damage along the corridor in recent decades. That is a statement about the creek corridor, not about every street in Roslyn.

What this means for your address

A mapped floodplain covers part of an area, not all of it. Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status is not verified on this page, so check your own address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center and ask the township how floodplain rules apply to your lot.

Homeowners policies usually exclude flood, and flood insurance is a separate policy. FEMA says flood policies typically have a 30-day waiting period, so buying cover during a storm warning may not help. Our flood damage cleanup page explains what happens when rising water enters a home.

First steps when water is in the house

Stop the source if you can do it safely, usually at the main shutoff where the line enters the house. Keep people and pets out of any room where water touches outlets, appliances or a panel, and call the utility or 911 about live wires.

Do not enter a flooded basement while the power is on, and do not run a household vacuum on standing water. If the water came up from a drain or a toilet, treat it as contaminated and wait for a crew. Photograph the damage before anything is moved.

How extraction and drying work

Extraction removes standing water with pumps and extraction units, and wet carpet pad or soaked insulation that cannot be dried comes out. Drying then uses air movers and dehumidifiers, and moisture meters guide the work until readings match a dry reference, which is the approach described in ANSI/IICRC S500, the published standard for water damage restoration.

Water also travels. It follows framing and drops through gaps, so a stain on a ceiling may come from a bathroom above, and a wet baseboard may mean water inside the wall. That is why a crew checks beyond the visible stain, and why burst pipe water damage and ceiling water damage repair are separate pages on this site.

Insurance and documentation

Take photos of the damage and the source, note the date and time you found it, and keep receipts for emergency steps. Contact your insurer promptly, because coverage depends on the cause of the loss and the terms of your policy.

The Pennsylvania Insurance Department advises making only the immediate repairs needed to prevent further damage and learning the full cost before settling. Moisture readings and an itemized scope from the restoration crew can help describe the work, but they do not guarantee that a claim will be approved.

Questions from Roslyn homeowners

Is Roslyn in Abington Township?

Most of it is. The community also extends into Upper Dublin Township, so check which township your address is in before asking about permits or utilities.

Does water damage in Roslyn mean my home is in a flood zone?

No. Most water damage starts with plumbing, appliances or roofs. Flood-zone status depends on your address, so check the FEMA Flood Map Service Center.

What should I do first if a pipe bursts?

Shut the water off at the main if you can reach it safely, keep people away from wet electrical areas and call. Photograph the damage before moving anything.

Does homeowners insurance cover water damage?

It depends on the cause and your policy. Sudden events are treated differently from long-term seepage, and flood is usually excluded, so ask your insurer.
