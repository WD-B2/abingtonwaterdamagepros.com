How to reach us

This site takes calls only. There are no forms to fill out, so nothing waits in an inbox while your floor is wet. Call the 24/7 emergency line, describe the situation and dispatch will take it from there.

For non-emergency questions, such as an estimate for repair after drying or a question about a service page, call as well and say that it is not urgent. For anything written, our email is listed in the footer.

What to have ready when you call

You do not need to know the cause. A few details help dispatch give better first steps and help the crew prepare.

Address and floor

Street address and which floor or room is wet.

The source

Pipe, appliance, roof, sewer or unknown, and whether it is stopped.

How much water

Standing depth, how many rooms and how long it has been there.

Materials

Hardwood, carpet, tile, drywall or plaster, and finished or unfinished basement.

Insurance

Whether you have notified your insurer, and any claim number.

Photos

Pictures taken before cleanup, if you can take them safely.
