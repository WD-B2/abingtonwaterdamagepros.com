Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information we collect when you call or write to Abington Water Damage Pros, and how we use it. Effective November 12, 2026.

24/7 emergency line

(445) 263-2078Call now

Who we are

Abington Water Damage Pros operates this website and a 24/7 phone line for water damage restoration in Abington, Pennsylvania. Our address is 1495 Old York Rd, Abington, PA 19001, our phone number is (445) 263-2078 and our email is contact@abingtonwaterdamagepros.com.

Information we collect

This site does not contain forms, so it does not collect information through the website itself. We collect information you give us when you call or email, such as your name, phone number, address, a description of the damage and details about your insurance if you share them.

Your browser and our hosting provider may also record routine technical data, such as the pages requested and the device type, as part of serving the site.

Call recording

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. If you do not want a call recorded, tell the person who answers at the start of the call.

How we use information

We use what you tell us to understand your situation, to send help, to write a scope of work and to follow up about your request. We may also use it to review the quality of our calls and to train the people who answer them.

Sharing

We share your information with restoration professionals who respond to your request, and with service providers that help run our phone line and website. We may share information when the law requires it, and we do not share your information for unrelated purposes.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you and ask us to correct or delete it, subject to legal and record-keeping duties. Write to contact@abingtonwaterdamagepros.com and give enough detail for us to find your record.

Security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold and keep it only as long as it is needed for the purposes above or as the law requires. No system is perfectly secure.

Children

This site is for adults seeking help with property damage and is not directed at children.

Changes

We may update this policy, and the effective date at the top will change when we do. Questions about it can go to the email above or to the 24/7 line.
