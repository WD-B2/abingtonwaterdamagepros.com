Musty Smell After Water Damage in Abington, PA: Causes and Fixes

A smell that lingers after the water is cleaned up is a signal. It usually means something is still damp. Here is how to find it and what to do.

  • By the Abington Water Damage Pros team
  • Published November 12, 2026

24/7 emergency line

(445) 263-2078Call now

The short answer

A musty smell after water damage almost always means moisture is still present somewhere, in carpet pad, behind a wall, under flooring or in insulation. Air fresheners and cleaners hide it without fixing it. Find the damp material with a moisture meter, dry or remove it and keep humidity low. If the smell comes from sewage or contaminated water, treat that as a cleanup job.

Health questions about odors belong to a physician. This page covers the building, not any medical effect.

Why the smell lingers

Odors come from materials that stay damp and from the microbial activity that moisture supports. Surfaces may look dry while the layer underneath is not, which is why the smell returns on humid days.

The cause is usually one of a handful of places.

Start with a simple search

Before you call anyone, try to narrow the location. Smells are strongest near their source, and a few steps will tell you a lot.

  1. 1

    Follow your nose

    Walk the room and note where the smell is strongest.

  2. 2

    Check the obvious

    Look under sinks, behind appliances and around windows for dampness or stains.

  3. 3

    Lift and look

    Pull back a corner of carpet or a cabinet panel to check what is under it.

  4. 4

    Measure humidity

    A cheap hygrometer shows whether the room stays above comfortable humidity.

  5. 5

    Take photos

    Record stains, soft spots and the date you noticed the smell.

A moisture meter is the most useful tool, and a crew uses a better one along with a thermal camera. If you do not have one, call for help when you find a damp area.

Drying is the fix

The solution is to dry and often to remove the material that cannot dry. Fans and a dehumidifier help in a small area. If the smell comes from a wall cavity, a subfloor or insulation, controlled drying with air movers and dehumidifiers is more effective than a household fan.

Our structural drying page explains how professionals dry hidden materials and verify the result. For flooring, see the carpet water damage restoration page, which explains why pad is usually replaced.

When the odor points to mold

A musty smell can mean mold growth, though only an assessment confirms it. If you see dark spots, the area has been wet for more than a day or two or the smell persists after drying, have it checked. Our mold remediation page explains how assessment, containment and verification work, following the ANSI/IICRC S520 standard.

Do not mask the smell with sprays, and do not paint over damp material, because the source stays.

Humidity and ventilation basics

The EPA's mold guidance says to fix leaks and other water problems as soon as possible and to dry items completely. It also advises scrubbing mold off hard surfaces with detergent and water, and considering a professional for large areas or contaminated water. Ventilation and moisture control are the lasting defenses against recurring odors.

Sewer and drain smells are different

A sewage smell is not a musty smell. If it comes from a floor drain, a toilet or standing water, it can mean a backup or a dry trap. Do not try to mop contaminated water yourself. See the sewage backup cleanup page for what to do, and call for help.

A dry floor drain can release sewer gas, and pouring water into it to refill the trap sometimes solves a faint smell. If the smell is strong or water is present, call.

Preventing the smell from coming back

Keep indoor humidity low with ventilation or a dehumidifier. Fix drips quickly, run bathroom fans and keep air moving behind furniture on cold walls. After any leak, dry the structure fully and not only the surface.

Smells after leaks in specific rooms

Basements hold odors from seepage and humidity, which our basement water damage page explains. Kitchens and baths hold smells from slow leaks under sinks and around tubs.

Nearby boroughs

Odors after water damage are common in older buildings everywhere. If your home is near the township, see our pages on Jenkintown and Hatboro.

Smells that point to a different problem

Not every musty odor means mold. A sour smell near a drain can be a dry trap, and a sweet or sharp smell near a furnace is a reason to call the gas utility or an HVAC tech. A damp, earthy smell that gets stronger when the weather is humid often means moisture in a basement or crawl space.

Track when the smell is strongest. If it follows rain, look at gutters, grading and window wells. If it follows showers, look at the fan and the ceiling below the bathroom. Those notes help a pro find the source faster.

Questions about musty smells in Abington

Why does my house still smell after the carpet dried?

The pad or subfloor may still be damp, or the smell may come from the wall cavity. A moisture meter can check.

Will a dehumidifier get rid of the smell?

It lowers humidity and helps, but it does not remove damp materials or hidden sources.

Is a musty smell always mold?

No. It means moisture is present, and only an assessment confirms mold.

Can I use an air freshener or ozone machine?

They mask odors and do not solve the moisture problem. Find and dry the source first.
