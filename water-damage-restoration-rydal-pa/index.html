Water damage in Rydal

Rydal lies within Abington Township and uses ZIP code 19046. It is mostly residential, with the Penn State Abington campus and a station on SEPTA's West Trenton Line as well-known landmarks. The community shares the Rydal-Meadowbrook Civic Association with Meadowbrook.

The township's planning documents mention Rydal for its steep slopes and for a stream corridor preserved through the Rydal Waters development. They do not describe flooding in Rydal, so this page does not claim any. That does not make a house immune from water damage, because most losses start with plumbing, appliances or a roof.

The usual causes inside the house

A supply line that fails behind a wall, a washing machine hose that splits, a water heater tank that corrodes through and a toilet that overflows are the most common ways water gets into a home. A leaking roof or a clogged gutter can send water through a ceiling.

Each has its own steps. For a failed fixture, close its shutoff. For a tank, shut the cold water supply to it. For a pipe, close the main. Our water heater leak cleanup, appliance leak water damage and burst pipe water damage pages explain what happens next.

Rental homes and student housing

Some people near a college live in rented space. If water is damaging a rental, tell the landlord or property manager in writing, photograph the damage and keep a copy of the message. The landlord's policy generally covers the building, and a tenant's renter's policy covers belongings, so each side should read its own coverage.

For landlords and property managers, a fast call limits the damage to neighboring units. Water moves through ceilings and shared walls, which is why dispatch asks which floor and which unit.

Safety first

Never step into water near live electrical equipment. Turn off power at the panel only if the panel is dry and you can reach it safely, and call the utility or 911 for anything that looks hazardous.

Water from a sewer or drain is contaminated and should be left to a crew. Do not use ordinary vacuums, and do not rely on household fans to dry a wet structure. Photograph everything before moving it.

What restoration involves

Crews work to the published ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration.

  1. 1

    Call

    Describe the water and get first-step guidance.

  2. 2

    Assess

    A crew checks moisture, finds the source and writes the scope.

  3. 3

    Extract

    Water comes out with pumps and extraction units.

  4. 4

    Dry

    Air movers and dehumidifiers run, and readings are tracked.

  5. 5

    Repair

    Drywall, flooring and paint are restored once everything is dry.

The scope and the price are explained before work starts. What the price depends on is set out on our structural drying page.

Questions worth asking before work starts

Before a crew begins, ask what the scope covers, which category the water falls into and how drying will be checked. You should also ask what could change the price, such as hidden water behind a wall or a larger wet area than first seen.

Ask what the crew will do if readings stay high, and who will tell you when the structure is dry. A clear answer to those questions tells you the work is planned, not improvised.

Insurance and documentation

Photos, dated notes and receipts for emergency steps help describe the loss. Contact your insurer promptly and ask what your policy says about the cause of the damage. The Pennsylvania Insurance Department advises making only immediate repairs needed to prevent further damage.

A restoration scope can help describe the work, but it does not guarantee that a claim is approved.

Questions from Rydal homeowners

What ZIP code is Rydal?

Rydal uses 19046, which is also the Jenkintown ZIP, but Rydal is in Abington Township and not in Jenkintown borough.

Is Rydal prone to flooding?

The township's planning documents do not describe flooding in Rydal. Check your address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center for your own risk.

I rent near campus and water is leaking. What should I do?

Tell the landlord in writing, photograph the damage and call if water is spreading. Keep copies of all messages.

Do you handle a ceiling that is sagging?

Yes. Stay out of the room, call and describe it. A crew finds the source and dries the space before repair.
