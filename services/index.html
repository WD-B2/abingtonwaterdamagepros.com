Find the right service by where the water came from

Every page below targets one situation, from a split supply line to a sewer backup. The groups mirror how crews think about a loss, which is source first, then material, then repair. If your problem touches two groups, read both pages, because many real losses do.

Pipes and plumbing

Water that starts inside a supply line, a tank or a pipe under the slab.

  • Burst Pipe Water DamageCleanup after a pressurized supply line fails, from shutoff to drying.
  • Frozen Pipe Water DamageWater damage from a pipe split by ice, which shows up at thaw.
  • Water Heater Leak CleanupCleanup after a water heater tank or fitting fails and water spreads from one spot.
  • Slab Leak Water DamageWater damage to floors and walls from a leaking water line under a concrete slab.

Appliances and HVAC

Leaks from machines, cabinets and cooling equipment.

  • Appliance Leak Water DamageWater from a failed appliance hose, valve, drain or overflow.
  • AC Leak Water DamageWater from an air conditioner's condensate line, drain pan or frozen coil.
  • Kitchen Water DamageWater damage to kitchen cabinets, the sink area, floors and walls.

Weather and building envelope

Water that arrives through the roof, the walls, the ground or the storm.

  • Ceiling Water Damage RepairStained, sagging or dripping ceilings, from any source above, including roof leaks.
  • Storm Water DamageWater that enters through the roof, windows or walls during a storm, not rising flood water.
  • Flood Damage CleanupRising water from creeks, groundwater or surface runoff, including contaminated water.
  • Basement Water DamageWater in the below-grade room, including sump failures, seepage and finished basements.
  • Crawl Space Water DamageWater and moisture in the under-floor crawl space, including insulation, vapor barrier and joists.

Water removal and drying

Getting standing water out and the structure dry.

  • Water ExtractionStanding water removed with pumps and extraction units, before drying starts.
  • Structural DryingDrying the structure with moisture mapping, air movers and dehumidifiers, also called water mitigation.
  • Sewage Backup CleanupContaminated water from a sewer or drain backup, with safety first.

Floors and walls

Repair work sorted by the material that got wet.

  • Hardwood Floor Water DamageWet, cupped or buckled wood and laminate flooring, including drying and repair choices.
  • Carpet Water DamageWet carpet and padding, including extraction, drying and replacement decisions.
  • Wall and Drywall Water DamageWet, stained or collapsed interior walls, drywall, plaster and baseboards.

Commercial

Offices, shops and multi-unit buildings.

  • Commercial Water DamageWater damage in businesses, offices, shops and multi-unit buildings, including downtime and documentation.

Mold

Growth that follows moisture the building held too long.

  • Mold RemediationFinding, containing and removing mold after moisture, including testing and clearance.

What every service page includes

Each page explains the first safe steps, how the crew works, what the estimate depends on and a short general note on insurance. None of them lists exact prices, because the scope of the water decides the price. We explain the scope and the price before work starts.

All of them point back to the same 24/7 line. A call lets dispatch hear the details and tell you which page and which steps apply.

What to do first while you call

Shut off the water or power only if it is safe, stay out of rooms where water meets electrical equipment and photograph the damage. Do not enter flooded basements or crawl spaces. The services here follow ANSI/IICRC S500, the published standard for water damage restoration.

Questions about our water damage services

Which service do I need if I am not sure what caused the water?

Call and describe what you see. Dispatch can help work out the likely source and the first steps, and the crew finds the rest with moisture meters.

Do you handle both water removal and repairs?

Yes. Crews extract and dry first, then repair drywall, flooring and finishes once the structure is dry.

Do you do fire damage?

Fire and smoke restoration are not part of this site. We handle water damage and the mold that follows it.

Does restoration include waterproofing?

No. Restoration dries and repairs. Stopping groundwater from entering is a waterproofing or drainage contractor's job.

Water spreading right now?

The line is open around the clock. Call, say what happened, and we will walk through the first steps.
